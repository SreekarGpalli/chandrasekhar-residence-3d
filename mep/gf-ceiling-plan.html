How the count was worked out IS 3646 Part 1

Each room needs a set light level in lux. The number of fittings is:

fittings  =  (lux needed × room area)  ÷  (lumens per fitting × UF × MF)

Our ceiling is 10'-6½" and there is no false ceiling, so the fittings are far from the floor and UF is only 0.38 to 0.53 — less than half the lamp's light lands where you need it. That is why the counts are what they are. A false ceiling at 9'-0" would need fewer fittings for the same lux.
